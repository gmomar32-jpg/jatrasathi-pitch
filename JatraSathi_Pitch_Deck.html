06 • Product & Anti-Fraud Innovation Slide 06 / 12

Zero-Dispute Architecture Built for Local Realities

Engineered specifically to prevent time-stalling, price haggling, and safety risks:

1. Two-Tier Smart Customer KYC

Public venue bookings (Hospital, Airport, Station) require only Name & Phone for instant booking. Private/long trips (Intercity, Day-Care, Abroad) enforce NID/Passport verification.

2. Customer "Work-Done Timer Lock"

Prevents companions from stalling at tea stalls to inflate overtime. As soon as work finishes, the customer taps "কাজ শেষ (টাইমার লক)" to freeze the billing timer immediately.

3. 4-Digit Start/End OTP & Free OSRM Maps

Trips start and finish only via customer OTP confirmation. Powered by OpenStreetMap + OSRM routing with BDT 0 monthly map API cost.

4. Live Modify & Official Digital Cash Memo

Displays Base Fare, live +Hour/+Day/Early-Return adjustments, 30% Online Advance deducted, and exact net Cash Due—eliminating verbal arguments.
